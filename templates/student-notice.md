# Information for students — template

Give this notice to your students before they use the chatbot (on the course page, or on a slide).
Replace each `TO BE COMPLETED: …` with your text, and have it checked by your institution's data
protection officer. You can translate it into your students' language.

---

## Using the course chatbot

**What it is.** A chatbot set up by your teacher for the course "TO BE COMPLETED: course name". It
answers from the course documents. It can make mistakes: check important answers in the course
documents.

**Your account.** You create it yourself when you open the link: click **Sign up** under the
sign-in form. You can use a nickname and an e-mail address that does not identify you (for example
`nickname7@class.local`; each address can be used only once). Choose a **new password**, not one you
use elsewhere: you need it again next time. Do not write personal information about yourself or
other people in your questions.

**Your grade.** TO BE COMPLETED (for example: your conversations do not count for your grade).

**What is stored, and where.**
- Your account and your conversations are stored in Open WebUI, in a computer space rented by your
  teacher: TO BE COMPLETED (for example: "a GitHub Codespace, a service of GitHub, Inc.").
- Each question you ask, with the parts of the course documents it needs, is sent to the company
  that runs the language model: TO BE COMPLETED (for example: "Anthropic"). It keeps this data for
  TO BE COMPLETED (look for "data retention" on its privacy page).

**Who can read your conversations.** Your teacher, as the administrator of the chatbot, can read
them. Other students cannot.

**How long.** Your account and conversations are deleted on TO BE COMPLETED (for example: at the
end of the semester).

**Questions or requests** (access, deletion): TO BE COMPLETED (teacher's or data protection
officer's contact).
