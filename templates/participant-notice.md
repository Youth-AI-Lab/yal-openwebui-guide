# Information for participants — template

Give this notice to the participants before they use the chatbot (on paper, on a slide, or on your
group's page). Replace each `TO BE COMPLETED: …` with your text, and have it checked by your
organisation's data protection officer. You can translate it into the language of your group.
For participants under 18, also check what your organisation asks of their parents or guardians.

---

## Using the chatbot of our group

**What it is.** A chatbot set up by TO BE COMPLETED: who (for example: "your youth workers") for
TO BE COMPLETED: the activity or topic. It is an artificial intelligence (AI), a computer
program, not a person. It answers from the documents we gave it. It can make mistakes: check
important answers in the documents, or ask us.

**Your account.** You create it yourself when you open the link: click **Sign up** under the
sign-in form. As your name, use a first name or a nickname, never your full name. As e-mail, use an
address that does not identify you (for example `nickname7@group.local`; each address can be used
only once). Choose a **new password**, not one
you use elsewhere: you need it again next time. Do not write personal information about yourself
or other people in your questions.

**What is stored, and where.**
- Your account and your conversations are stored in Open WebUI, in a computer space run by
  TO BE COMPLETED: who (for example: "your youth worker, on GitHub Codespaces, a service of
  GitHub, Inc.").
- The link goes through GitHub (a service of GitHub, Inc.), which sees that you open it. The first
  time, click **Continue** on its warning page.
- Each question you ask, with the parts of our documents it needs, is sent to the company that
  runs the language model: TO BE COMPLETED: its name (for example: "Anthropic, in the United
  States"). It keeps this data for TO BE COMPLETED: how long (for use through an API key, the
  guide gives the page of Anthropic to check).
- When you click a thumb (up or down) under an answer, your rating, your comment if you write one,
  and a copy of the conversation are kept for TO BE COMPLETED: who (for example: "your youth workers"), to improve the chatbot. They
  can also see how many questions each account asked.

**What the chatbot remembers.** TO BE COMPLETED: one of these two sentences (for example:
"Nothing: each conversation starts from zero.", only if Memory is turned off in every chatbot,
including new ones; or: "It can remember what you tell it about yourself and use it in your next
conversations, with all the chatbots of the group. You can see and delete it in Settings →
Personalization.")

**What you can do yourself.** You can delete a conversation (its menu **…** → **Delete**), and
what the chatbot remembers about you (your name at the bottom left → **Settings** →
**Personalization**). Leave the profile photo, the bio, the gender and the birth date of your
account empty: the chatbot does not need them.

**Who can read your conversations.** TO BE COMPLETED: who (for example: "your youth workers"), as
the administrators of the chatbot, can read them. The other participants cannot.

**How long.** Your account and conversations are deleted TO BE COMPLETED: when (for example: "on
30 June" or "at the end of the project").

**Questions or requests** (access, deletion): TO BE COMPLETED: a contact (youth worker or data
protection officer). Your questions are processed outside the European Union (for example, in the
United States): TO BE COMPLETED: on which basis (your data protection officer fills this in). You
can also complain to TO BE COMPLETED: the data protection authority of your country.
